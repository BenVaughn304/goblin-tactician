# Goblin Tactical Engine

## Overview
The Goblin Tactical Engine is a robust system designed to enhance gameplay experiences in tactical scenarios, allowing players to engage in strategic combat with goblins.

## Features
- **Dynamic Combat Mechanics**: The engine utilizes a variety of combat factors, including terrain, unit type, and strategic positioning to influence the outcome of encounters.
- **Flexible Unit Customization**: Players can customize goblin units to suit their strategies, enhancing replayability.
- **AI Integration**: Intelligent AI provides challenging opponents that adapt to player strategies.
- **Multiplayer Support**: Engage in tactical battles with friends or online players.

## How It Works
The Goblin Tactical Engine operates through a series of algorithms that simulate strategic combat scenarios:
1. **Initialization**: Players set up their goblin units and select the combat environment.
2. **Combat Simulation**: The engine calculates outcomes based on combat factors such as health points, attack strength, and defense mechanisms.
3. **Resolution**: Players receive real-time feedback on combat outcomes, enabling them to adjust their strategies accordingly.

## Combat Factors
- **Terrain Modifiers**: Different terrains may provide advantages or disadvantages.
- **Unit Types**: Each goblin type has unique strengths and weaknesses.
- **Player Strategy**: Tactical decisions significantly influence the combat results.
- **Random Number Generation**: The engine utilizes RNG to add unpredictability to combat outcomes.

## Usage Instructions
1. **Start the Game**: Launch the Goblin Tactical Engine.
2. **Create or Select a Goblin Squad**: Choose from various goblin types or create your own.
3. **Set Combat Parameters**: Decide on terrain and opponents for the battle.
4. **Engage in Combat**: Execute your strategy and witness dynamic combat scenarios.
5. **Review and Learn**: Analyze combat results to refine strategies for future encounters.

## Conclusion
The Goblin Tactical Engine offers an engaging and tactical gameplay experience that challenges players to think strategically. With its rich feature set, players can immerse themselves in a world of goblin warfare, and leverage the power of strategy to overcome their adversaries.
